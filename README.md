## 👋 Hi, I'm Wojciech

I've spent **13 years building web systems** — as an engineer, tech lead and creator of my own SaaS products.

By day I lead a team at an international tech company, building subscription and purchase flows for the largest Scandinavian media (VG, Aftonbladet, Aftenposten). The rest of the time I run **[Delvero](https://delvero.pl)** — a studio that brings AI and automation into small and mid-sized companies and ships its own SaaS products, used daily by paying customers.

Technology is just a tool. What matters is the process that stops eating your time.

<br />

## 🤖 How I work with AI

Most of my code is written **with AI agents, not instead of them**. I treat Claude Code as a teammate: I plan, decompose and review; agents implement, test and verify.

- 🛠️ **Building my own tooling** — a custom multi-agent orchestrator for Claude Code (model routing, parallel workers, review gates) plus a library of skills, hooks and MCP servers that encode how my repos actually work
- 🧭 **Onboarding engineering teams** into AI-assisted development and agentic workflows — what Delvero does for clients, I first tested on myself and my own teams
- ⚙️ **Shipping AI into products** — from AI photo restoration to document pipelines and company-data verification APIs exposed over MCP
- 📏 **Opinionated about guardrails** — contract-first APIs, type-checks and lint as the agent's safety net, review before merge, no "vibe" in production
